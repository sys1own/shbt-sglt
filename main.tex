\subsection{POSIX Shared-Memory SPSC Transport}
\label{sec:zero_copy_telemetry}

High-rate telemetry is transported through two unidirectional POSIX shared\footnote{POSIX Shared-Memory Telemetry Architecture: \url{https://github.com/sys1own/shbt-cf}}
memory rings: a driver-to-\shbtos{} acquisition ring and a
\shbtos{}-to-user-daemon publication ring.  Each object is created with
\texttt{shm\_open}, sized once with \texttt{ftruncate}, and mapped into the
participating address spaces with \texttt{mmap(..., MAP\_SHARED, ...)}.  The
hardware driver maps DMA destination pages directly onto producer-owned frame
slots, and the consumer reads the committed slot in place.  No payload copy,
serialization pass, system-call transition, or heap allocation occurs in the
steady-state data path.

Each ring is a power-of-two array of $Q$ telemetry frames with monotonic
producer and consumer indices
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
h&=\texttt{write\_head},\qquad
t=\texttt{read\_tail}, \\
n_{\mathrm{used}}&=h-t,qquad
n_{\mathrm{free}}=Q-(h-t), \\
i_{\mathrm{write}}&=h\mathbin{\&}(Q-1),qquad
i_{\mathrm{read}}=t\mathbin{\&}(Q-1).
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:spsc_ring_indices}
\end{equation}
The producer owns \texttt{write\_head}; the consumer owns
\texttt{read\_tail}.  Both are C-ABI-compatible \texttt{AtomicUsize} values.
The producer writes a complete frame and publishes $h+1$ with release ordering.
The consumer loads $h$ with acquire ordering, validates the frame, processes it
in place, and publishes $t+1$ with release ordering.  Full and empty states are
defined by $h-t=Q$ and $h=t$, respectively.  Unsigned monotonic-index
subtraction makes wraparound well defined for ring occupancies smaller than
half the index space.

The verified end-to-end transfer latency from producer release to consumer
acquire is
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
t_{\mathrm{shm}}&=0.561\;\mu\mathrm{s}, \\
t_{\mathrm{shm}}&<1.000\;\mu\mathrm{s}.
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:posix_shm_latency}
\end{equation}

The frame type and both index records use strict cache-line layout equivalent
to \texttt{\#[repr(C, align(64))]}.  The write-head record, read-tail record,
and frame array begin on separate $64$-byte cache lines.  Consequently, the
producer never writes the consumer's cache line and the consumer never writes
the producer's cache line, eliminating false sharing and cache-line bouncing
from the SPSC synchronization path.

\subsection{SECDED ECC and Constant-Time Channel Remapping}

Every persistent $64$-bit SRAM payload shall be stored as a Hamming $(72,64)$ SECDED codeword.  Seven Hamming check bits occupy the power-of-two positions in the first $71$ codeword locations, and the seventy-second bit supplies overall parity.  For received codeword $\bm c$, the decoder forms the seven-bit syndrome $s$ and overall-parity mismatch $p$ and applies
\begin{equation}
\begin{array}{ccl}
s=0,\ p=0 &\Rightarrow& \text{no error},\\
s\neq0,\ p=1 &\Rightarrow& \text{correct one indexed bit},\\
s=0,\ p=1 &\Rightarrow& \text{correct the overall-parity bit},\\
s\neq0,\ p=0 &\Rightarrow& \text{detect double-bit error}.
\end{array}
\label{eq:secded_decision_table}
\end{equation}
Correction shall occur before a payload enters the Active Visible Register.  A double-bit detection shall inhibit the affected command, latch \texttt{ECC\_SYNDROME}, and invoke channel replacement rather than release corrupted state to the transducer array.

Dynamic replacement is performed by \texttt{shbt\_remap}, which applies an AVX-512 Givens rotation to the failed and spare channel components.  For channel indices $i$ and $j$,
\begin{equation}
\begin{bmatrix}x_i'\\x_j'\end{bmatrix}
=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta&-\sin\theta\\
\sin\theta&\cos\theta\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix}x_i\\x_j\end{bmatrix}.
\label{eq:givens_channel_remap}
\end{equation}
The selected coefficient pair is loaded from a fixed table, and masked permutes replace the selected lanes without scanning the channel set.  One vector load, one fixed shuffle/multiply-add schedule, and one vector store therefore give $O(1)$ execution with respect to the number of active channels.  The routine shall preserve the vector norm to the fixed-point rounding bound and shall commit \texttt{REMAP\_SRC} and \texttt{REMAP\_DST} before the replacement channel is enabled.

\begin{equation}

+\frac{ar^2-a^3}{2f_0^2}
+\mathcal O\!\left(\frac{r^4}{f_0^3}\right).
\label{eq:eikonal_path_series}
\end{equation}
In the commanded steering plane, $a=r\sin\Theta_{\mathrm{tilt}}$, so that
$r^2-a^2=r^2\cos^2\Theta_{\mathrm{tilt}}$.  Applying the compensating phase
$\phi_{\mathrm{eikonal}}=-k_0W$ produces the high-order command
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}

&-\frac{r^2a}{2f_0^2}\cos^2\Theta_{\mathrm{tilt}}
+\mathcal O\!\left(\frac{r^4}{f_0^3}\right)\biggr], \\
k_0&=\frac{2\pi}{\lambda_0},\qquad
\Theta_{\mathrm{tilt}}=\sqrt{\Theta_x^2+\Theta_y^2}.
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:nonparaxial_eikonal_phase}
\end{equation}

$r^2a\cos^2\Theta_{\mathrm{tilt}}/(2f_0^2)$ supplies the pre-distortion that
cancels the dominant off-axis coma.  The phase map is evaluated at every
emitter coordinate $(x_m,y_n)$ and combined with the tensor-correction phase

\subsection{Diffraction-Limited Optical Baseline}

At the reference impact radius $r_0=1.000$ m, the unobscured synthetic lens has an effective aperture
\begin{equation}
D_{\mathrm{eff}}=2r_0=2.000\;\mathrm{m}
\label{eq:effective_aperture}
\end{equation}
at the baseline focal distance $f_0=169.30$ m.  For monochromatic observations at $\lambda=500$ nm, the Rayleigh angular resolution is
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\theta_{\mathrm{res}}
&=1.21967\frac{\lambda}{D_{\mathrm{eff}}} \\
&=1.21967\frac{500\times10^{-9}\;\mathrm{m}}{2.000\;\mathrm{m}} \\
&=3.049\times10^{-7}\;\mathrm{rad} \\
&=0.06289\;\mathrm{arcsec}.
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:rayleigh_resolution}
\end{equation}
This value defines the ideal reconstruction-grid scale before detector sampling, formation error, finite bandwidth, and residual metric-phase error are folded into the system point-spread function (PSF).

The on-caustic coherent amplitude magnification is specified by the circumference-to-wavelength ratio
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\mu_{\mathrm{amp}}
&=\frac{2\pi r_0}{\lambda} \\
&=\frac{2\pi(1.000\;\mathrm{m})}{500\times10^{-9}\;\mathrm{m}} \\
&=1.2566\times10^7.
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:caustic_magnification}
\end{equation}
Expressed as an astronomical magnitude gain,
\begin{equation}
\Delta m=2.5\log_{10}\mu_{\mathrm{amp}}=+17.75\;\mathrm{mag}.
\label{eq:magnitude_amplification}
\end{equation}
The quoted gain is the ideal peak caustic value; science-product calibration shall apply the finite-source, spectral-bandpass, detector, and caustic-centering transfer functions.

Using the closed-loop phase-jitter bound $|\delta\phi|\leq5.05\times10^{-5}$ rad as the rms allocation gives the phase-only result

&>S_{\mathrm{ver}}=0.999999984.

\label{eq:strehl_acceptance}

\subsection{Regularized Modified Richardson--Lucy Reconstruction}

Let $\bm y\geq0$ be the background-subtracted focal-plane photon counts, $\bm x\geq0$ the unknown celestial intensity distribution, $\bm H$ the spatially variant caustic-response operator, and $\bm b\geq0$ the estimated dark-current and stray-light background.  The forward Poisson model is
\begin{equation}
\bm y\sim\operatorname{Poisson}\!\left(\bm H\bm x+\bm b\right).
\label{eq:poisson_caustic_model}
\end{equation}
The response operator shall be generated from the measured wavelength-dependent PSF and normalized such that $\bm H^{\mathsf T}\bm 1$ captures edge truncation and nonuniform exposure.  Starting from a strictly positive flat-field or back-projected estimate $\bm x^{(0)}$, iteration $k$ forms
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\bm r^{(k)}
&=\frac{\bm y}{\bm H\bm x^{(k)}+\bm b+\varepsilon}, \\
\bm c^{(k)}
&=\frac{\bm H^{\mathsf T}\bm r^{(k)}}
{\bm H^{\mathsf T}\bm 1+\varepsilon}, \\
\widetilde{\bm x}^{(k+1)}
&=\bm x^{(k)}\odot\bm c^{(k)},
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:modified_rl_core}
\end{equation}
where division and $\odot$ are elementwise and $\varepsilon>0$ prevents division by zero.  A nonnegative Laplacian-regularized, relaxed update suppresses caustic ringing and noise amplification:
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\bm x^{(k+1)}=\mathcal P_+\!\bigl[&
(1-\beta)\bm x^{(k)}+\beta\widetilde{\bm x}^{(k+1)}\\
&-\tau\bm L^{\mathsf T}\bm L\bm x^{(k)}\bigr],
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:regularized_rl_update}
\end{equation}
where $0<\beta\leq1$ is the relaxation factor, $\tau\geq0$ is selected from calibration data, $\bm L$ is the discrete gradient operator, and $\mathcal P_+$ projects negative samples to zero.  After projection, each iterate is flux-normalized to the background-corrected count estimate:
\begin{equation}
\bm x^{(k+1)}\leftarrow
\bm x^{(k+1)}
\frac{\max\!\left(\bm 1^{\mathsf T}(\bm y-\bm b),0\right)}
{\bm 1^{\mathsf T}\bm H\bm x^{(k+1)}+\varepsilon}.
\label{eq:rl_flux_normalization}
\end{equation}
Iteration terminates when both the relative image change and relative Poisson deviance change fall below their calibrated tolerances, or when the fixed iteration ceiling is reached:
\begin{equation}
\frac{\|\bm x^{(k+1)}-\bm x^{(k)}\|_2}
{\|\bm x^{(k)}\|_2+\varepsilon}<\epsilon_x,
\qquad
\frac{|\mathcal D_{k+1}-\mathcal D_k|}
{\mathcal D_k+\varepsilon}<\epsilon_D.
\label{eq:rl_stopping_rule}
\end{equation}
Reconstruction products shall carry the PSF version, regularization parameters, iteration count, residual deviance, photometric normalization, and propagated formation/metrology covariance needed for reproducibility.
